# Medialane

**Better rails for creators, collectors, businesses and builders.**

Medialane is where creative work becomes programmable intellectual property: created, licensed, collected and traded onchain, by its creators and on their terms. We are building the open foundation for **creator capital markets**, where a song, a film, a photograph, a research paper or a whole archive can be released, licensed and backed directly by the people who value it.

[medialane.io](https://medialane.io) has been live on Starknet mainnet since March 2026, and it keeps growing with new services and upgrades.

---

## What you can do on Medialane

**Create and protect.** Publish any work (art, music, video, photography, writing, documents, software) and record its authorship, date and license terms permanently with the asset. Every asset carries its license and its AI policy wherever it goes.

**Launch.** The Creator Launchpad turns a work into a release: single editions, limited editions, collections, timed drops, remixes, creator coins, badges, event tickets, membership clubs and sponsorships.

**Collect and trade.** The marketplace lets anyone list, buy, make offers and check out a whole cart at once, with full provenance on every asset.

**Remix and license.** Request a license to build on someone else's work. Attribution and royalties flow back to the original creator.

**Build.** Every feature in our apps is available to developers and AI agents through an open SDK and public API.

## What we stand for

- **Creators own their work and its terms.** Authorship is public and permanent. Wallets are self-custody from the first moment.
- **Open and permissionless.** Anyone can create, list, buy or build, and needs nobody's approval to do it.
- **The chain is the record.** Ownership, trades and provenance live onchain, where anyone can verify them.
- **Interoperable by default.** Assets follow open standards and work with wallets, explorers and marketplaces beyond Medialane.
- **Licensing that travels.** Terms are written into each asset's metadata in a format other platforms already read. Creative Commons BY-SA is the default.
- **Integrity over hype.** Rewards follow real participation, and discovery is never ranked by trading volume alone.
- **Built for people and agents.** AI agents use the same open interfaces as everyone else.
- **Public authorship, private economics.** We keep what is public and minimize the data we hold about you.
